Playbook: find your best performing platform

Most creators spread themselves thin because they cannot tell which platform actually sends people anywhere. Here is a simple way to find out in a week.

  • Create one tracked link per platform pointing at the same destination.
  • Share each link only on its own platform for seven days.
  • Compare clicks and unique visitors per link. The winner is where your audience actually acts.
  • Shift more effort to that platform and keep the others on maintenance.

Playbook: track a product launch

A launch is a campaign, not a post. Treat it like one and you will finally know what worked.

  • Open a campaign named after the launch.
  • Build a tracking link for each placement with the UTM builder, then shorten it.
  • Tag every post and link to the campaign as you publish across the week.
  • After launch, read one scoreboard for total clicks, visitors, and revenue, then compare it to your last launch.

Playbook: clean up your link in bio

Your bio is prime real estate. Make every tap count.

  • Replace raw URLs with branded tracked links so you can see what your bio actually drives.
  • Tag the bio link with its own source so it does not get mixed up with post traffic.
  • Review it monthly and point it at whatever you most want people to do next.
