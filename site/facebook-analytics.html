The gap native Facebook insights leave open

Facebook is one of the few places where your posts and your page can carry links directly, which makes it a strong driver of traffic. The catch is measurement. Native Facebook insights are built around reach, reactions, comments, and shares. They tell you how many people saw a post and how many engaged with it, but they go quiet on the question that matters most for your business: did that post send a real visitor to your site, and did that visitor turn into a signup or a sale?

That is the gap Schedlytics closes. Instead of guessing which post drove a busy traffic day, you place a tracked link in the post itself and in the links on your page. Every click is recorded with the post and campaign it came from, so you can line up reach against clicks against signups and finally see which content earns its place.

An honest note on connections: you can track Facebook traffic today using tracked links in your posts and on your page, and that works right now with no waiting. A direct Facebook account connection inside Schedlytics, which will pull native Facebook stats automatically, is rolling out and is awaiting platform approval. Native Facebook stats are coming soon, so the click attribution below is available today while the deeper account connection lands.

Tracked links in your posts and on your page

A tracked link is a short, branded link that records every click along with where it came from. Drop one into a post about a new product, put one in your page bio, and add one to the link buttons on your page. From that moment on, each tap is logged with its source and campaign. You do not need a native connection for any of this, because the link itself does the measuring. See how it works under link tracking, and build a clean, consistent link with the UTM builder so every Facebook link follows the same naming you use everywhere else.

Attributing clicks to a specific post or campaign

The real power shows up when you give each post or each push its own tagged link. A link tied to your Tuesday product post stays separate from the one in your weekend promo, so when traffic spikes you know exactly which post earned it. Bundle several posts into a single campaign and Schedlytics rolls the clicks up for you, which makes a full launch easy to read at a glance. The same approach carries across every channel you use, so Facebook sits next to your other sources in one view through social media analytics and campaign tracking.

Comparing organic posts fairly

Reach can be misleading. A post that reached fifty thousand people but sent forty clicks did less for your business than a quieter post that reached eight thousand and sent three hundred clicks. When you measure by clicks rather than by reach alone, your best performers stop hiding behind vanity numbers. Schedlytics puts reach next to clicks for each post, so an organic post that quietly converts gets the credit it deserves and earns a repeat in your content plan.

Connecting Facebook to revenue

Clicks are the bridge between a post and a sale. Because every tracked link ties a click back to the post it came from, you can follow that visitor through to the signup or purchase on the other side. Now a Facebook post is not just a reach number in an insights panel. It is a clear line from content to clicks to revenue, so you know what each post is actually worth and where to spend your next hour of effort.
