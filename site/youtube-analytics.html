The gap YouTube Studio leaves open

YouTube Studio is great at what it does. It shows views, watch time, retention, and where your audience comes from. What it cannot show you is what happens after someone leaves the video. Did that tutorial actually sell your course? Did the review send buyers to the product? Studio goes quiet at exactly the moment that matters for your business.

Schedlytics fills that gap. By putting a tracked link in your description or pinned comment, every click is recorded with the video and campaign it came from. Now you can line up "12,481 views" next to "742 clicks" and "38 signups," and finally know which videos earn their place in your content calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Does Schedlytics replace YouTube Studio?

No. Studio shows views, watch time, and audience data. Schedlytics adds the missing piece: the clicks your videos send to your links, so you can connect a video to the traffic and revenue it produced.

How does it track clicks from a video?

You place a Schedlytics tracked link in your video description, pinned comment, or card. Every click is recorded with its source and campaign, so traffic ties back to that exact video.

Do I have to connect my YouTube account?

Connecting YouTube lets Schedlytics pull live channel and video stats alongside your click data. You can also use tracked links on their own without connecting an account.

Does this work for Shorts?

Yes. Any video where you can place a link, including Shorts with a pinned comment, can be tracked the same way.
