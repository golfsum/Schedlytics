Privacy Policy

Last updated June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Schedlytics ("Schedlytics", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our scheduling and analytics application and website (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

This document is a starting template. Please review it with a qualified legal advisor and replace the placeholder contact details and company information before publishing.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and profile details you choose to provide.

Connected social accounts

When you connect a platform (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Twitch, or Patreon) through its official OAuth login, that platform gives us a limited access token. We use it to read the data you authorize, which may include your public profile, follower and view counts, post and video statistics, and engagement metrics. We never receive or store your social account password.

Content you create

We store the posts, captions, media references, schedules, and links you create inside the Service so we can publish and display them for you.

Usage data

We may collect basic technical information such as browser type, device, and pages visited to keep the Service secure and reliable.

2. How we use information

  • To provide scheduling, publishing, and analytics features you request.
  • To display your performance metrics from connected platforms.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To communicate with you about your account and support requests.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • With the social platforms you connect, in order to read your statistics or publish the content you ask us to publish.
  • With service providers who help us run the Service (for example hosting), under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

4. Platform data and compliance

Our use and transfer of information received from platform APIs follows each provider's requirements, including the Google API Services User Data Policy (including the Limited Use requirements), the Meta Platform Terms, the TikTok Developer Terms, and the Pinterest Developer Guidelines. We only request the minimum permissions needed for the features you use.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can disconnect any platform at any time, which removes the stored access token for that platform. You can also request full deletion of your data (see the section below).

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access, update, or correct your account information from the Settings screen.
  • Disconnect any connected platform at any time.
  • Revoke our access directly from each platform's app or security settings.
  • Request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Data deletion

You can request deletion of all data we hold about you on our Data Deletion page, or by emailing privacy@schedlytics.app. We will process valid requests promptly.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the date above.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@schedlytics.app.
