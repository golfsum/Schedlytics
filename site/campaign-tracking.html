Judge the campaign, not the post

When every post lives on its own, it is easy to fool yourself. One reel goes viral and feels like a win, while the quiet email that actually drove the sales gets no credit. Campaign tracking fixes that by rolling every piece of content behind a goal into a single view, so you can see what the effort earned as a whole.

In Schedlytics you tag posts and tracked links with a campaign. From then on, every click flows into that campaign automatically. You get a clean scoreboard for the launch, promotion, or season, and a fair way to compare one campaign to the next.

Frequently asked questions

What is campaign tracking?

It groups all the posts and links behind one goal so you can measure the whole effort together, instead of judging each post in isolation. Schedlytics ties clicks, traffic, and revenue to the campaign that produced them.

Do I need UTM parameters?

UTMs help, and Schedlytics can add them for you. You can also tag any tracked link with a campaign name directly, with or without UTMs.

Can one campaign span several platforms?

Yes. A campaign spans every post and link you assign to it, across YouTube, Pinterest, and the rest, so you see one combined result.

Can agencies track campaigns per client?

Yes. Use a campaign per client initiative to keep results clean and easy to report on.
