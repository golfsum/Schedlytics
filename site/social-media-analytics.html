Stop guessing which posts are working

Every platform gives you a different dashboard, a different definition of a "view," and no way to tell whether any of it sent a single visitor to your website. That makes it almost impossible to answer the one question that matters: which content is actually growing your business?

Schedlytics is built around that question. You shorten your campaign links, share them in your posts and bio, and the platform records every click, the platform it came from, and the campaign it belongs to. Instead of a wall of vanity metrics, you get a clear picture of what drives real outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

What is social media analytics?

It is the practice of measuring how your content performs across platforms. Schedlytics goes further than likes and views by tracking the clicks each post sends to your links, so you can see which content drives real traffic and revenue.

How is this different from the analytics inside each app?

Native dashboards stop at the platform. They cannot tell you whether a post sent anyone to your website or store. Schedlytics follows the click across the gap, so you can attribute traffic and revenue back to the exact post.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Pinterest, Twitch, and Patreon are available today, with Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok rolling out as platform reviews complete.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes tracked links and basic analytics. Paid plans start at 9 dollars per month for unlimited links and campaigns and a 90 day history.
