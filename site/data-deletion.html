Data Deletion

Last updated June 22, 2026

You can ask Schedlytics to delete all data we hold about you at any time. This includes your account details, the access tokens for any platforms you connected (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest), and the posts and schedules you created.

Option 1: Request deletion here

Enter the email address linked to your Schedlytics account and submit the form. We will remove your stored data and the access tokens we hold for your connected platforms, then send a confirmation.

Option 2: Email us

You can also email privacy@schedlytics.app from your account email with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm once your data is removed.

Option 3: Revoke access at the platform

You can remove Schedlytics access directly from each platform. This immediately stops our access to that account:

  • Instagram and Facebook: Settings, then Security, then Apps and Websites.
  • YouTube and Google: myaccount.google.com, then Security, then Third party access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions.
  • Pinterest: Settings, then Apps, then Connected apps.
What gets deleted: your account record, all stored OAuth access and refresh tokens, your cached profile and stats, and the posts, captions, schedules, and links you created. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
