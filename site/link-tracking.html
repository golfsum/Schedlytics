A link tracker built around content attribution

A plain short link tidies up a long URL and stops there. A tracked link keeps working after the click. It tells you how many people followed it, how many of them were new, which platform sent them, and which campaign they belong to. That turns a link from a convenience into a measurement tool.

Use tracked links in captions, YouTube descriptions, newsletters, campaign posts, and your link in bio. Give each piece of content its own link and you can compare the posts and platforms that actually send traffic. Those results feed your social media analytics so scheduling supports attribution instead of being the whole product.

Frequently asked questions

What is a trackable link?

It is a short link that records every click. Schedlytics captures the click count, unique visitors, source platform, and campaign so you can see which links drive real traffic.

Can I use Schedlytics as a free link tracker?

Yes. Start with tracked links for social posts, videos, bios, or campaigns, then use the same dashboard to compare the content and platforms that send traffic.

What is the difference between clicks and unique visitors?

Clicks count every tap, including repeats. Unique visitors count distinct people. Comparing the two tells you whether a link reached many people or the same few several times.

Can I use my own branded domain?

Yes. Business plans support custom branded domains so your short links carry your name instead of a generic one.

Do tracked links work with UTMs?

Yes. Add UTM parameters to any tracked link for deeper attribution, or build them quickly with the UTM builder.
