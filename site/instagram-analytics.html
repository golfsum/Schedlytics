The gap Instagram Insights leaves open

Instagram Insights is good at what it does. It shows reach, impressions, saves, shares, and a breakdown of who your audience is. What it cannot show you is what happens after someone taps your bio link or a story link sticker. Did that reel actually send buyers to your shop? Did the carousel grow your newsletter? Insights goes quiet at exactly the moment that matters for your business, the moment a follower turns into a visitor.

Schedlytics fills that gap. Instagram sends traffic in two main ways: the link in your bio and link stickers in stories. By putting a tracked link in either spot, every tap is recorded with the source and campaign it came from. Now you can line up reach against clicks, and a quieter post that converts gets the credit a viral one with no link never earned. You can do all of this today with tracked links, no account connection required.

One honest note. A direct Instagram account connection in Schedlytics is rolling out and is awaiting platform approval. That connection will pull native follower and post stats next to your click data. Until it lands, you are not waiting on anything: tracked links in your bio and story stickers already give you the traffic picture Insights leaves out, and native Instagram stats are coming soon.

Tracked links in your bio and your stories

The link in bio is the single most valuable piece of real estate on your Instagram profile. It is where every reel, every post, and every story caption points people. When you make that link a Schedlytics tracked link, you turn a plain destination into a measurable one. If you run a link-in-bio landing page with several buttons, you can track each button on its own and see which offer your audience actually taps. Our link in bio strategy guide walks through how to set that up so nothing goes unmeasured.

Stories are the other half of the story. A link sticker on a story is often the most direct call to action you have, and it disappears in twenty four hours, so knowing whether it worked is the difference between a habit worth repeating and a guess. Drop a tracked link into the sticker and Schedlytics records every tap with its source, so a story you almost did not bother posting can prove it was your best traffic driver of the week. Attribute clicks to a specific post or campaign

Reach alone cannot tell two posts apart once the traffic arrives. A tracked link can. By tagging each link with a campaign, you trace a click back to the exact reel, carousel, or story that started it, instead of crediting your profile as a whole. Promoting a product drop across a launch reel, a few stories, and a pinned post? Group them into one campaign and measure the entire push together. Build clean, consistent tags fast with the UTM builder, so every link you share is ready to be measured.

From profile visit to action, and then to revenue

Most Instagram growth advice stops at profile visits. The real question is what happens next: how many of those visitors clicked through, and how many of those clicks turned into a signup or a sale. Schedlytics follows that chain. You can see which content drives profile visits that lead to action, not just attention, and connect a sale or a newsletter signup back to the post behind it. That is how you find out what each reel is genuinely worth, rather than ranking your content by likes. For the bigger picture across every channel you post on, see social media analytics.
